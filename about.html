01 — STORIA

Storia e Heritage

Fureco, an Italian company owned by the Gavazzi family, has supported Italian craftsmanship for over a century, transforming the finest furs into exclusive luxury garments. Each creation expresses a deep respect for tradition and a constant passion for innovation — values that define the essence of the Fabio Gavazzi collections. A modern company, representing the elegance of Made in Italy around the world, whose greatest strength lies in its ability to reinterpret and personalize tradition to create unique and distinctive pieces.

02 — ARTIGIANATO

Artigianato e Lusso Italiano

Fabio Gavazzi furs are crafted using the finest skins, carefully selected by our expert artisans. Every stage of production — from tanning to dyeing, from design to patterns, and the entire manufacturing process — is carried out entirely in Italy, following meticulous procedures that ensure exceptional quality, comfort, and lightness. With our exclusive customization service, it is possible to create a one-of-a-kind, handcrafted fur garment, tailor-made to reflect your personal style.
