Exclusivity in the heart of Milan

Fabio Gavazzi creations are available at the prestigious Milan showroom on Via Montenapoleone 23, housed in the eponymous Palazzo Gavazzi, a historic building dating back to the early 19th century. Here, you can experience the elegance of Made in Italy and the remarkable versatility of handcrafted garments, with a high level of personalization and bespoke design. Visit our Milan showroom to explore the latest collections and enjoy a truly personalized luxury experience.
