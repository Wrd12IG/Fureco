“La prima azienda italiana ad adottare Furmark®”
02 — IL NOSTRO APPROCCIO

Fureco was the first Italian company to adopt Furmark®, the international certification for natural fur. The company follows a strict sourcing policy based on transparency and respect for animal welfare, aligned with the highest international environmental standards, ensuring that each garment is made with certified, traceable, and responsibly sourced fur. This standard provides customers with the certainty of choosing authentic furs and verifying their origin, in full compliance with Fureco’s ongoing commitment to responsible luxury.
